What it does not do

  • Outbound rules work on IPv4 addresses, not names. An approved domain opens its whole IP: with only example.com approved, a step reached www.cloudflare.com on the same address.
  • The secret scan does not stop encrypted data (A24). The guarantee is that the VM holds no key and has no route out, which with an approved domain means no route other than that IP.
  • A task takes about 2 minutes, nearly all of it VM boot. A microVM starts far faster.
